1. Bill of materials

Counts are AE2 parts and blocks. Their crafting cost depends on your pack's recipes (check JEI). Colour cables with dye in a crafting grid (8 cables + 1 dye).

2. Build it layer by layer

  1. Start at layer 0 and work up. On each layer, place the cable cells first, with all their parts, then the controllers.
  2. Put each part on the side shown. Hold the part and right-click that side of the cable, or right-click the neighbouring controller's face.
  3. Parts on a cell's up side face the next layer: place them before you build that layer.
  4. Every controller face next to a cable cell needs a part: a tunnel (or a cable anchor, if shown). An empty face merges the two networks.
  5. Use the cable colour shown. Touching cables of different colours don't connect, which keeps each group of at most 8 tunnels separate. Fluix (dense) collectors connect to every colour that touches them, by design.
  6. Exit sides stay open. That's where your tunnel network's cable connects.
